# SUPPLY-CHAIN-ANALYSIS
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing business performance, KPIs, trends, and insights using data visualization and analytics.
Home Page

* Provides a complete overview of the dashboard in a single view
* Displays important KPI cards such as Total Sales, Profit, Orders, and Revenue
* Includes navigation buttons for easy movement between pages
* Helps users quickly understand overall business performance
* Designed with an interactive and user-friendly layout

 Sales Performance Page

* Analyzes overall sales and revenue performance
* Shows monthly and yearly sales trends using charts and graphs
* Compares profit and revenue growth over time
* Identifies peak sales periods and declining trends
* Allows filtering by category, region, and time period for deeper analysis

 Customer Insights Page

* Provides analysis of customer purchasing behavior
* Identifies top customers contributing to revenue
* Shows customer segmentation and buying patterns
* Helps understand customer retention and repeat purchases
* Supports better marketing and customer engagement strategies

 Product Performance Page

* Highlights top-performing and low-performing products
* Analyzes sales and profit contribution by product category
* Tracks product demand and quantity sold
* Helps identify profitable products and improvement areas
* Supports inventory and pricing decision-making

 Regional Analysis Page

* Displays business performance across regions or locations
* Uses maps and charts for geographical analysis
* Compares sales and profit between different regions
* Identifies high-performing and low-performing markets
* Helps in planning regional business strategies and expansion
 Operational and Supply Chain Insights Page

* Monitors order processing and delivery performance
* Tracks shipping status and operational efficiency
* Identifies delays and bottlenecks in the supply chain
* Analyzes shipping modes and delivery trends
* Helps improve logistics and customer satisfaction

 KPI and Trend Analysis Page

* Tracks important business KPIs and growth metrics
* Compares current and previous performance trends
* Displays trend analysis using interactive visuals
* Helps identify business growth patterns over time
* Supports data-driven strategic decision-making

 Interactive Filters and Drill-Through Analysis

* Includes slicers and filters for customized analysis
* Allows users to explore data dynamically
* Enables drill-through functionality for detailed insights
* Improves dashboard interactivity and user experience
* Helps users perform flexible and detailed data analysis
